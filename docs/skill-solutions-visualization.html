00 · the paradigm

You have a problem.
Here is the machine that solves it.

Scroll through problems, not categories. Each problem proposes ways to solve it, and each way resolves into a Solution Skill — an ordered chain of the skills this library already contains, with the hand-off between them made explicit and every skill attributed to its own author.

01 · problem

"I have a vague idea and need shippable code."

The most common arrival state: a founder or lead with a one-line idea and no spec, no tickets, no build. Getting this wrong means weeks of work on the wrong thing.

02 · ways to solve it

Three routes. One trade-off each.

Not one prescriptive answer — a choice. The reader sees the cost of each route before committing.
